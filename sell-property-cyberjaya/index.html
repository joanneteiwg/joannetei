How we compare relevant evidence

Comparable does not mean identical.

We prioritise properties that are genuinely useful comparisons, then account for differences instead of relying on a single headline price. The result is an indicative assessment for discussion—not a formal valuation.

  1. 1
    Same development or nearby micro-market
    Location, neighbourhood character and immediate competing supply.
  2. 2
    Similar property fundamentals
    Property type, land and built-up size, floor level, orientation, tenure and layout.
  3. 3
    Condition and differentiators
    Renovation, furnishing, view, lot position, privacy and other material features.
  4. 4
    Current competition and available evidence
    Relevant asking prices, available transaction references and present buyer response.
