Landed Viewing Checklist

What to Inspect Before Buying a Subsale Landed Home in Cyberjaya

Cyberjaya Properties | Wolf Group

On a second viewing, spend as much time outside the house as inside it. A fresh coat of paint can improve the first impression, while issues such as roof leaks, poor drainage or uncertain extension paperwork can affect what you spend after getting the keys. Use the same checks for every terrace, superlink, semi-D or bungalow on your shortlist.

Inspect the roof, walls and ground

Where to lookWhat to check
Upper-floor ceilings and roof edgesWater stains, peeling paint or recent patching around roof junctions and gutters. Ask when the issue first appeared and what was repaired.
Bathrooms and adjoining wallsDamp patches beside wet areas and on ceilings below them. With the owner’s permission, run the taps and check how quickly water drains.
Side garden and rear yardWhere water is directed, whether drains are accessible and whether there are signs of pooling along the house or boundary.
Front porch and roadWhether your cars fit with the gate shut, whether the driveway slope is practical and where guests can park.
Doors, frames and wallsRepeated cracks in the same area or doors that stick. Ask an inspector or engineer to assess concerns before committing.

Match the extension and side land to the documents

If the owner extended the kitchen, enclosed a terrace or added a covered structure, request the available approved plans and approval records. Compare what’s shown with what’s built. For a corner or end lot, ask your lawyer to confirm the parcel boundaries and the extent of private land. Tell your lawyer about any structure or fenced area that appears to cross those boundaries. Majlis Perbandaran Sepang provides application routes for minor renovation and amendments or additions to building plans. Majlis Perbandaran Sepang: Building Control Department

If the house is in a landed strata scheme, ask management what rules apply to the renovation you intend to do. Verify what part of the outdoor space and driveway belongs to the parcel and what areas are shared. Land and Mines Department: Strata titles and common property

Turn your viewing notes into an offer budget

Sort the work by timing. Identify repairs needed before moving, jobs you can do in the first year and optional upgrades. Get quotes for items that could change your offer, such as roof work or fixing recurring damp. Ask for any repair promises to be dealt with in writing through your lawyer. Add the immediate repair allowance to your purchase budget before comparing the house with another Cyberjaya listing.
