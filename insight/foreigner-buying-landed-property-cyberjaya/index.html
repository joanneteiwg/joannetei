Can Foreigners Buy Landed Property in Cyberjaya? What to Check Before You Offer

Cyberjaya Properties | Wolf Group

Yes, a foreign individual can apply to buy an eligible landed strata home in Cyberjaya. Cyberjaya is in Sepang, which falls in Selangor’s Zone 1. Under the standard state rules, the minimum for an eligible residential purchase is RM2 million. A landed house on an individual land title generally falls outside the standard foreign-purchase route, even if it costs more than RM2 million. The exact property and buyer are still subject to the state approval process. Selangor Land and Mines Office: Foreign acquisition and Circular 1/2014 · Invest Selangor: Foreign property requirements

How much should a foreign buyer budget for stamp duty?

From 1 January 2026, the Stamp Act applies an 8% residential transfer duty to a buyer who is neither a Malaysian citizen nor a Malaysian permanent resident. The duty is calculated on the higher of the purchase consideration or market value. For example, if the duty is assessed at RM2.2 million, the transfer duty is RM176,000. Budget separately for any financing duty, legal fees, valuation, consent-related charges and bank or currency conversion costs. A Malaysian permanent resident falls outside this particular 8% rule for foreign individuals, while Selangor still includes permanent residents in its consent process. HASiL: Stamp Act 1949, First Schedule, item 32(ab) · Selangor Land and Mines Office: Section 433B

What should you do before paying a deposit on a resale?

  1. Have a Malaysian conveyancing lawyer check the exact property. Provide the lot or parcel details, price and your buyer status. If the strata title has yet to be issued, ask what documents establish the scheme and what the situation means for your purchase.
  2. Confirm financing or proof of funds. Ask your lender what margin it can offer for this purchase and which documents it needs for overseas income. If you are paying in cash from overseas, confirm the remittance schedule and bank requirements.
  3. Get the consent and refund terms in writing. Before committing, ask your lawyer to review who holds the deposit, the deadline for obtaining consent and what happens to the deposit if consent is refused or cannot be obtained in time.

Does MM2H lower the Cyberjaya minimum?
MM2H has its own programme price requirements. Have your lawyer check Selangor’s state threshold, eligible title type and consent requirements for the exact purchase, even if your MM2H category has a lower programme minimum.
