Selling a House in Cyberjaya: How to Price It and Work Out What You’ll Keep

Cyberjaya Properties | Wolf Group

Before you put your Cyberjaya house on the market, set a realistic asking price and estimate your net proceeds. Use comparable sales to work out what buyers might pay. Then deduct your loan settlement and selling costs to see how much you could have available after the sale.

Which Cyberjaya transactions should you compare?

Start with recent completed sales in the same development and, where available, the same precinct and house type. Compare a three-storey intermediate superlink in Symphony Hills with similar superlinks first. A corner home with side land or a semi-D needs its own comparison. For Setia Eco Glades, check the enclave and lot position before using a neighbouring sale as evidence.

Record the transaction date, house type, land size, built-up size, tenure, lot position and known condition. Review active asking prices separately to see which other homes buyers will find. If you can only find a few close matches, look over a longer period or at comparable developments and note what differs. Use district-level figures for wider market context. How much might you receive after the sale?

Use a realistic sale price to estimate your proceeds. Ask your lender for an indicative settlement figure, then have your lawyer confirm the formal redemption amount and timing during the sale. Allow for your agreed agency fee, legal charges, outstanding property bills and any applicable tax or temporary retention.

What should you prepare before the first viewing?

  • Property documents. Gather your purchase agreement, title or assignment documents where applicable, recent property bills and management statements. Flag known title restrictions or consent issues.
  • House facts. Confirm land and built-up areas and identify alterations. Gather approvals or renovation records where available.
  • Occupancy. For a tenanted home, have the agreement ready and check viewing arrangements, the tenancy end date and what you can commit to on handover.
  • What stays. List the fittings and appliances included. Photograph the actual property in daylight, including the areas buyers are likely to compare.

Ask your lawyer which documents and consents apply to your transaction. Malaysian Bar: Subsale conveyancing guidance

When should you review the asking price?

Set a review date when you launch. If the listing gets plenty of online views but few qualified enquiries, look at the price, photographs and property details together. If several buyers raise the same concern during viewings, consider whether the listing should explain it better or the price should reflect it. Compare written offers on price, financing, conditions and timing before accepting.
