Trace a normal day through the plan

Start at the car porch and follow the route to the kitchen with groceries. Imagine doing laundry, putting children to bed and hosting relatives. Mark every activity that requires stairs. If an older parent will stay, check the route from the porch to their intended room and bathroom, including steps, door widths and where you could install grab rails if needed.

Decide which rooms need daytime quiet. A study near the living area can work for casual use. If you spend hours on calls, check whether a room on a different level gives you the separation you need. Check the plan dimensions against the furniture you intend to bring.

Compare the exact lot before choosing an upgrade

Look at the lot position, facing, usable outdoor space and any price difference. Ask for written details of planned facilities and applicable charges. For a family that needs easier access, the placement of a bedroom and bathroom can make a bigger difference than a larger total floor area.
