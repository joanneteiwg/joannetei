New Launch vs Subsale in Cyberjaya: Which Landed Home Fits Your Timeline?

Cyberjaya Properties | Wolf Group

When comparing a new landed launch with a completed subsale house in Cyberjaya, start with the date you need the keys and the cash you’ll spend by that point. A completed home lets you inspect the actual rooms, street and surroundings. A home under construction gives you time before moving, but you’ll need a plan for where you’ll live and what you’ll pay while waiting.

What should you verify with a developer?

Use the official TEDUH portal to check the developer licence and advertising and sales permit for the exact project or phase, where applicable. Ask to see the payment schedule and the draft Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA). The National Housing Department says housing developers covered by the applicable housing regulations cannot collect payments before the SPA is signed. Ask your lawyer which rules apply to the purchase you’re considering. TEDUH licence and permit search · National Housing Department enforcement FAQ

When comparing packages, separate the actual property price from incentives. Ask what is included for your selected lot, which fees are covered and when the lender expects payments to begin.

Which option works if your move date is fixed?

Get a realistic key date for each option before ending your current tenancy or promising a move. With a resale home, ask whether it is vacant, owner-occupied or tenanted. Have your lawyer explain the completion conditions and whether the agreed handover can meet your timeline. With a launch, ask how long you’ll need after key collection before the home is furnished and ready. Malaysian Bar: Subsale conveyancing guidance
