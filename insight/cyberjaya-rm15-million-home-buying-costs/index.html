How Much Cash Do You Need to Buy a RM1.5 Million Landed Home in Cyberjaya?

Cyberjaya Properties | Wolf Group

Budget about RM232,000 for the main upfront costs in this example. It assumes a Malaysian citizen buys a RM1.5 million subsale landed home in Cyberjaya, takes a RM1.35 million loan and pays standard stamp duty and undiscounted legal scale fees. Keep a separate reserve for other charges, repairs and moving.

If the lender values the same house at RM1.4 million and lends 90% of that figure, the buyer needs RM90,000 more toward the price. Check whether you could cover that gap before you make an offer.

The transfer duty breaks down into RM1,000 on the first RM100,000, RM8,000 on the next RM400,000, RM15,000 on the next RM500,000 and RM20,000 on the final RM500,000. The example uses RM1.5 million for duty purposes. The standard transfer bands apply to the higher of the agreed consideration or market value. The legal fee rows use the First and Third Schedule scales. Stamp Act 1949, First Schedule · Solicitors’ Remuneration Order 2023

Ask your lawyer and lender for itemised quotes covering valuation, searches, registration, subsidiary documents, applicable taxes and insurance. These are separate from the illustrated subtotal, as are renovation and moving. Ask your lawyer how any earnest deposit is credited toward the price and when each payment falls due.

What if the lender’s valuation is lower?

Some lenders calculate the financing margin using the lower of the purchase price and their assessed market value. Ask your lender which value it uses, what margin you qualify for and which costs it can finance. AIA Malaysia home loan product brief

The RM90,000 measures the change in the buyer’s contribution to the price. Loan-related fees may also change with a smaller loan. If the gap would use up your repair budget or emergency reserve, check financing for the exact property early and have your lawyer review the offer terms.

Set a budget for the house and the work it needs

Compare each property with its move-in costs. An older terrace with roof or waterproofing work due can require more cash early on than a well-maintained home at a slightly higher price. If it’s in a managed development, get the current charges and add them to your monthly budget alongside the loan instalment.

Do I need the full amount when I make an offer?
Payments fall due at different points. Ask for a schedule showing the earnest deposit, any balance deposit, cash required before loan disbursement and the legal and financing bills.
